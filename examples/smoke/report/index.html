Interpretation and limits

Candidate values, effective dates, and authority are supplied annotations. This experiment tests bounded memory updates, not arbitrary information extraction. Human label review, real model comparisons, adapter training, external validation, and generalization claims remain separate milestones.

The two passes preserve different questions: can the proposer choose the right operation, and does its own accumulated memory remain correct?
